HanziWeave

Learn Mandarin by reading your own English books

HanziWeave is an Android app that reads your DRM-free English epub books aloud or on screen, and weaves in the Mandarin words you are learning, with pinyin. Start from zero or pick the words you already know, and your Chinese grows as you read.
